Frequently Asked Questions

1. What game development services does DICO Studio provide?

We specialise in 3D modeling, animation, co-development, optimisation, and porting services for PC, console, and mobile.

2. What is the typical cost structure for outsourcing game development to DICO Studio?

Our cost structure is flexible and tailored to project scope, milestones, or dedicated team hourly rates.

3. What security measures are in place to protect our project's confidentiality?

We sign strict NDAs and employ secure, siloed data servers to protect all intellectual properties.

4. Can I outsource 3D modeling or animation separately?

Yes, you can hire our teams separately for specific asset production pipelines or full-cycle needs.

5. What platforms and engines does DICO support?

We fully support Unreal Engine, Unity, and build games for PlayStation, Xbox, Switch, PC, and Mobile.

6. How do we collaborate with your team?

Through dedicated project managers, daily sprint updates via Slack/Discord, and regular milestone reviews.

7. How do we get started?

Simply click the "Get In Touch" button, share your game's brief, and we'll arrange a discovery call.
