About Dico Studios

DICO Studio is a leading game development outsourcing company, providing AAA-quality game art,

animation, and co-development services for top studios worldwide.

Our Offices

With offices in Tokyo, Germany, and Spain, we integrate seamlessly with game developers, delivering cutting-edge 3D art, technical solutions, and full-game production support.

With a decade of experience, we’ve collaborated with industry leaders such as Bandai Namco, Square Enix, and Konami, bringing innovative, high-performance gaming experiences to global audiences.
